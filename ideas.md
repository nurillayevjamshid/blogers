# Dizayn yo‘nalishi

## Tanlangan yo‘nalish: MIO Calm Operations
Kundalik influencer operatsiyalarini yengil va ishonchli ko‘rsatadigan, oq fonli, yumshoq coral aksentli boshqaruv paneli.

- **Design movement:** editorial operations dashboard — ko‘p ma’lumotni tinch, tartibli qatlamlarda ko‘rsatish.
- **Principles:** ikki tabga fokus, jadvalda aniq ustunlar, kuchli status ranglari, ortiqcha navigatsiyasiz tezkor amallar.
- **Color philosophy:** ivory/white surfaces, slate text, coral primary, emerald completed, amber reminder.
- **Layout:** yuqorida brand + user session, darhol ikki tab, keyin tabga xos toolbar va responsive table/card.
- **Interaction:** modal oynalar, 44px touch targets, viewer uchun disabled actions, nickname link uchun Instagram ikonka.
- **Animation:** qisqa fade/slide; reduced-motion holatida o‘chadi.
- **Typography:** system sans / Inter fallback, kuchli sarlavhalar va ixcham uppercase metadata.
- **Brand essence:** MIO Beauty va MIO Home hamkorliklarini bitta tartibli ish stoliga yig‘ish.
- **Voice:** sodda, aniq, o‘zbekcha.
- **Wordmark/logo:** mavjud MIO harf belgisi va brand chiplar qayta ishlatiladi.
- **Signature color:** #F0826D coral.
